# LNReader third-party plugins notice

English | [简体中文](NOTICE-LNReader.md)

The novel source packages under `catalog/packages/lnreader.*` wrap pre-built plugins from upstream [LNReader/lnreader-plugins](https://github.com/LNReader/lnreader-plugins) (`plugins/v3.0.0` branch). Upstream plugins are licensed under the **MIT License** (full text in [LICENSE-LNReader.txt](LICENSE-LNReader.txt)), and each wrapped package and `catalog/source.json` entry preserves upstream author, URL, and converter version metadata.
